## What helps

- Same company description, address and phone on every profile (`offsite.md`).
- Facts stated plainly on the site: prices, timelines, what's included (already on service pages, `/pricing` and `/llms.txt`).
- Published guides that answer the prompt directly in the first two or three sentences.
- Reviews on Google Business Profile, Clutch and GoodFirms.
